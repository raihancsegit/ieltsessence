BEFORE EVALUATION Band 5.5

Sample Student Submission:

"In recent time, technology is very important for many people. It has many advantages but also bad impact on society. I agree that people use internet too much."

⚠️ Weakness: Repetitive vocabulary, informal tone, lacking complex cohesive structures.
AFTER MENTOR FEEDBACK Band 7.5

Improved Band 7.5 Version:

"In contemporary society, technological advancements play a pivotal role in daily life. While digital connectivity offers undeniable benefits, excessive reliance on the internet poses significant societal challenges."

✅ Enhancement: Academic vocabulary, refined coherence, and complex grammatical accuracy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

আমাদের কোর্স ও সার্ভিস সম্পর্কিত সাধারণ জিজ্ঞাসাগুলোর উত্তর খুঁজে নিন।

1. How long does it take to prepare for IELTS? ▼
Depending on your current English proficiency level and target score, preparation typically takes 40 days to 4 months. We conduct a Diagnostic Assessment to determine your exact timeline.
2. Can beginners join IELTS ESSENCE? ▼
Yes! Our 'IELTS Basic To Advanced' course is specifically designed for beginners to build English grammar and vocabulary fundamentals before moving to exam strategies.
3. Do you provide Writing Evaluation? ▼
Absolutely. Every student gets detailed line-by-line evaluation for Task 1 and Task 2 essays according to official Cambridge assessment criteria.
4. Is Speaking Practice available daily? ▼
Yes. Our Speaking Partner Program allows students to practice daily with matched partners and receive regular feedback from mentors.
5. Do you offer online classes? ▼
Yes, we offer live interactive online classes with HD recording access as well as physical offline classes at our SM Bhaban, Badda campus.
6. Do you provide Mock Tests? ▼
Yes, we conduct full-length paper-based and computer-delivered mock tests with comprehensive score analytics and post-test counselling.
7. Can I get counselling before admission? ▼
Yes, free 1-on-1 expert counselling is available anytime! Simply click 'Free Counselling' or contact us on WhatsApp to book a slot.
8. Which course is best for me? ▼
Take our Diagnostic Assessment or talk to our lead counsellor to find the exact course program tailored to your target band score and deadline.
